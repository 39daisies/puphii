At a glance

  • Puphii has no account. Your puff logs, plan, settings, savings calculator details and Bubble Break scores stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • If you use or buy Puphii Pro, Apple processes the payment and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives the purchase and device details needed to confirm your access. RevenueCat stores this information in the United States. It does not receive your puff logs or other app data.
  • You can ask us to delete the subscription records RevenueCat holds (section 6).
  • Puphii has no advertising, tracking or data sales. Apart from the subscription information in section 6, we don’t collect information about how you use the app.
  • No name, email address, account or payment details are requested to use the website or share a poll answer. Submitted poll answers are kept only as aggregate totals.
  • Hosting providers for the website and this site may process IP addresses and other technical information.

The operator is the person responsible for protecting personal information at Puphii, including handling privacy requests and complaints, and can be reached at the same address.

Puphii works without an account. What you enter is stored only on your device. Puphii doesn’t send it to us or anyone else: there’s no account, cloud sync, advertising or analytics.

Information from Apple

Apple distributes the app. Apple’s handling of your App Store account, downloads and purchases is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Apple gives developers statistics about their apps, such as downloads, sales and App Store page views. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may also give us crash reports and aggregated usage information about Puphii. Apple says this information doesn’t identify you personally.

4. Deleting app data

Because the app’s information stays on your device, you control it there. In Puphii’s settings, open Data Management:

  • Clear puff history permanently deletes your puff logs, along with your plan’s milestones and the days you confirmed as puff-free. Your plan settings, other settings, savings calculator details and Bubble Break scores stay.
  • Reset everything returns your logs, plan, settings, savings calculator and Bubble Break record to their defaults and turns reminders off. It keeps only the date the app was first opened and its data format version. It doesn’t affect a Puphii Pro subscription or the RevenueCat customer ID (section 6).
  • Deleting the app removes everything Puphii stored on your device.

We don’t hold a copy of this information, so we can’t recover it after deletion.

5. Reminders

Daily reminders are optional. Puphii asks for notification permission only when you turn reminders on. Reminders are scheduled on your device, and nothing is sent to a server. The reminder text doesn’t mention smoking or vaping.

6. Puphii Pro subscriptions

Puphii Pro subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple through your Apple Account. Puphii never sees your card or payment details.

Puphii uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States) to check whether you have Puphii Pro, show subscription prices and free-trial eligibility, and restore purchases. Puphii connects to RevenueCat each time you open the app or switch back to it, and when you open Puphii Pro or buy or restore a subscription. This happens whether or not you subscribe.

What RevenueCat receives

  • A RevenueCat customer ID. It is random, created on your device, and not linked to a name, email address or account. It normally stays the same until you delete the app. If you reinstall Puphii and restore purchases, RevenueCat may link the new ID with the earlier one.
  • Your device’s identifier for vendor (IDFV). Apple assigns this ID to all apps from the same developer on your device. It resets when you delete all of that developer’s apps.
  • Device and app details: device model, iOS version, Puphii version, preferred languages and your App Store country.
  • Purchase information from Apple: which subscription you bought, its price and currency, transaction IDs, purchase, renewal and expiry dates, trial or introductory-offer status, and the App Store receipt.
  • Tracking permission status. This is your device’s App Tracking Transparency status. Puphii never asks for tracking permission.
  • Your IP address, as with any internet connection. RevenueCat says it uses the IP address to estimate your country and does not keep the IP address itself.
  • Technical information about RevenueCat’s software, such as its version and whether its requests succeed, which RevenueCat may use to run and troubleshoot its service.

These identifiers do not include your name or contact details. They are persistent, though, so the information is pseudonymous, not anonymous: in RevenueCat’s dashboard we can see a customer’s purchase history under these IDs, as well as totals across all customers, such as the number of subscribers and revenue.

What RevenueCat does not receive

  • Your puff logs, plan, savings details, settings and game scores. They stay on your device.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA). Puphii does not collect it, does not ask for tracking permission, and does not share data with advertising or attribution services.
  • Puphii does not use RevenueCat’s paywall, advertising or attribution features.

Why

Only to provide the subscription: show what can be bought, confirm what you bought, and give you access. The information is not used for advertising or to track you across other companies’ apps or websites. RevenueCat processes this information as our service provider, under its data processing agreement with us. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Where

RevenueCat stores this information on Amazon Web Services servers in the United States.

Cancelling and restoring

You can manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone’s Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions. See Apple’s guide, Cancel a subscription from Apple. Deleting Puphii doesn’t cancel a subscription.

Restore Purchases, in Puphii Pro, restores access to an active subscription bought with the same Apple Account (Terms of Use, section 5.6).

Deleting subscription records

Deleting Puphii removes the customer ID from your device, but not the records RevenueCat holds. To ask us to delete them, email puphiicount@gmail.com. Puphii has no accounts and the app doesn’t display the customer ID, so we need your help to find your records:

  • If you bought a subscription, include the order ID shown on your receipt from Apple and the approximate purchase date. We use them to look for your records.
  • If you never bought a subscription, your records contain no purchase history and are linked only to the random ID on your device. We usually can’t find them, so we may not be able to act on the request.

Please don’t send card details, passwords or your Apple Account sign-in details. We will tell you what we found and what we did.

When we find your records, we delete them through RevenueCat. RevenueCat describes deletion as permanent and completes it in the background. Its privacy policy says it may keep personal information under its retention policy where needed to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce its agreements, and its data processing agreement refers to its standard backup and archival practices.

Deleting these records doesn’t cancel your subscription, so cancel it with Apple first. If you keep using Puphii, the app creates a new record at RevenueCat the next time it checks your subscription.

The app’s Learn section links to public health sources such as cdc.gov, smokefree.gov and nhs.uk. They open in your browser only when you tap them. Those sites receive a normal web request, such as your IP address and browser details, and may set cookies under their own policies. Puphii doesn’t add any of your information to the link.

8. No advertising or tracking

Puphii has no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting services, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not sell personal information.

Because we don’t track visitors across websites, we don’t treat browser “Do Not Track” signals differently.

9. The Puphii website and polls

This section covers the Puphii educational website and optional polls described in the notice of October 12, 2026. See also section 2 (Q3).

  • No name, email address, account, or payment details are requested to use the website or share a poll answer.
  • Your current, unsubmitted poll selection stays in the page until you submit it.
  • Submitting a poll sends your selected option to our server, which adds it to an aggregate total.
  • Our application database does not store a visitor IP address or individual poll-response record. Hosting providers may still process IP addresses and other technical information.

9.1 Before you submit

Selecting an option in the browser does not send it to the poll database. Submission occurs when you press “See how others feel” after selecting an answer. “Just see results” and “Skip this question” do not submit an answer. No spending calculator, puff counter, or health assessment is used on the polls page.

9.2 Optional poll answers and interest votes

The current polls ask whether quitting or cutting back is on your mind and what kind of encouragement you would like. Earlier versions asked about starting smoking or vaping and current goals. These topics may be personal or sensitive. Participation is optional; you can skip either question or view results without answering. When you submit, the selected option travels to the server so it can increment a daily response total.

The application stores only a date-and-option key and a count. It does not create a visitor ID or store a response linked to your identity, IP address, other answers, or spending figures. Poll totals are shown to visitors. This does not mean every part of the hosting system is anonymous.

We also store daily aggregate counts of page loads and poll interactions. Older totals can include spending-calculation reveals and interest-button clicks from earlier versions of the experience. We use these counts to understand whether the experience is useful and whether people express interest in Puphii. They are event counts, not verified unique people. Repeat visits, additional sessions, bots, and blocked requests can affect the totals.

9.3 Browser session storage

Your browser’s session storage remembers the choice you submitted to each poll and whether you clicked the interest button. This discourages repeat submissions in the same tab. These local flags are not sent as visitor identifiers. They may remain through reloads in that tab; clear session storage to remove them. Closing the tab normally ends the session, but browser restoration behavior can vary.

9.4 IP addresses, hosting logs, and cookies

The website runs through ChatGPT Sites and its hosting infrastructure, including Cloudflare. A hosting provider can receive your IP address when serving pages or receiving votes. Providers may also process browser or device information, request times, requested pages, cookies, and security or operational logs to deliver, protect, and operate their services.

We do not save IP addresses in the poll database or use IP-based profiles for poll results. We cannot promise that hosting providers do not retain IP addresses or other logs. Their collection and retention are governed by their services and privacy practices, and we do not independently control every provider log.

We have not added advertising pixels, fingerprinting, cross-site analytics, or app-created tracking cookies. This is not a claim that the hosting platform is cookie-free. More information: OpenAI Privacy Policy and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

9.5 Who can see poll information

Visitors can see aggregate poll-choice totals. The operator can see aggregate site and interest totals on an access-restricted results page. Providers process technical information to operate their services. We do not sell poll answers or share them for advertising profiles.

9.6 Poll retention and requests

Aggregate counts are retained while useful for evaluating the experience. The application cannot locate or remove a specific person’s submitted vote because it does not retain an individual response identifier. Please consider that limitation before submitting. It does not restrict any legal rights you may have concerning identifiable information held elsewhere.

13. Where information is processed

The operator is based in Japan. Our providers are based in, or process information in, other countries:

  • RevenueCat stores subscription information in the United States (section 6).
  • Apple, Google and GitHub are based in the United States and may process information in other countries.
  • For the website’s hosting providers, see section 9.4.

Information may therefore be processed outside the province, state or country where you live, including in the United States and Japan. Privacy laws differ between countries, and courts, law enforcement and other authorities in those countries may be able to access information under their laws. Contact us if you need more information about the handling relevant to your use of Puphii.

Japan

Because the operator is based in Japan, Japan’s Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI) may apply to how we handle personal information. You can contact us at puphiicount@gmail.com to ask for disclosure, correction, deletion or suspension of use of personal information we hold about you, to ask about our security measures, or to make a complaint. We designed Puphii so that habit data stays on your device, and we don’t run our own servers for the app.

14. How long information is kept

  • App data on your device: until you delete it in the app or delete the app.
  • Subscription records at RevenueCat: until we delete them at your request (section 6) or our RevenueCat account ends. We haven’t set a separate time limit. RevenueCat may keep some information after deletion, as described in section 6.
  • Poll and site totals: while useful for evaluating the experience (section 9.6).
  • Emails: as needed to handle the conversation and relevant obligations.
  • Provider logs: under each provider’s own practices.
