3. What Puphii is, and isn’t

Puphii is a habit-awareness tool. You tap once each time you smoke or vape, and a small pufferfish reacts. You can also set an optional reduction plan, estimate spending and play a short game.

Puphii’s reactions are playful feedback. They are not a measurement of physical harm, a diagnosis or medical advice. Puphii is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Plan paces and targets are examples you choose and can change. They are not clinical recommendations.

If you’re worried about your health, please talk to a healthcare professional.

You can delete your data at any time in the app’s Data Management settings. The Privacy Policy describes what each option removes, and how to ask us to delete subscription records held by our subscription provider.

Other features are free, including logging puffs and undoing a log, today’s count, the current week’s overview and history chart, viewing and deleting your logs, the reduction plan, Bubble Break, reminders and settings.
