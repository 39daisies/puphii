# Puphii — App Privacy Policy (publication draft; NOT APPROVED)

**Prepared:** 19 October 2026. **Status:** Owner and legal review required before publication. This draft describes intended release behavior; reconcile against the final native build.

## 1. Who operates Puphii
Puphii (the “App”) is operated by Naoko Hamasaki, trading as **39 Daisies**, Oita Prefecture, Japan (“we”, “us”). Contact: **puphiicount@gmail.com**. 39 Daisies is a trade name, not an incorporated entity.

This policy concerns the Puphii iOS app, not the Puphii website, waitlist, polls, or the separate Quope app. The initial App Store distribution is intended for the United States, Australia, New Zealand, and United Kingdom.

## 2. Data you provide and store locally
The App records information you choose to enter: puff and skip timestamps, consumption preferences, baseline and reduction-plan settings, savings estimates, reminder settings, and app preferences. The Bubble Break game stores gameplay information. These records are stored on your device, not in a Puphii cloud account.

**Voice Pep Rally (launch feature, pending native verification):** If you choose to record a message, the App requests microphone access and stores the resulting audio and its metadata in app-private local storage. Recording is optional. We do not intend to upload, transcribe, sell, or use these recordings for AI training. Only record another person's voice with their permission. The final implementation must be checked for microphone permission, media location, backup exclusion, and deletion before these statements can be published as verified facts.

## 3. Apple subscriptions and RevenueCat
Purchases and renewals are processed by Apple. RevenueCat manages subscription entitlements and may process an app/customer identifier, transaction and purchase information, subscription status, and device/app metadata required by its SDK. We do not receive your payment card number. We do not intentionally send puff/skip history, reduction-plan content, or voice recordings to RevenueCat. See https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ and https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/.

**Verification required:** confirm actual RevenueCat identifiers, SDK collection, processor terms, data location, retention, and App Store privacy-label declarations.

## 4. Permissions and other services
Microphone permission is requested only when you choose to record a Voice Pep Rally (subject to native implementation verification). Notifications are optional and scheduled locally for reminders. Opening an external educational link sends you to the destination website, which has its own privacy practices. We do not intentionally include third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs; verify the final release dependencies.

## 5. Purposes and legal grounds
We process subscription identifiers and transactions to deliver and verify paid access, administer purchases, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations. Where UK GDPR applies, the relevant legal grounds may include performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, and legitimate interests in service security and support. The precise lawful-basis mapping must be reviewed against actual processing before publication. Device-only content is handled locally by the App and is not available to us as a hosted content database.

## 6. Retention, deletion, and security
You can delete individual local records where supported, clear history, and use Reset Everything. Deleting the App ordinarily removes its app-private local data, subject to operating-system backup behavior. Apple and RevenueCat may retain purchase records under their own policies and legal obligations. We cannot retrieve locally stored audio after it is deleted. We use platform protections and access controls appropriate to the data processed. **Verify** whether Pep Rally audio is excluded from backups and whether all deletion paths remove files as expected.

## 7. International processing
We operate from Japan. Apple and RevenueCat may process subscription-related information outside your country, including in the United States. We will provide applicable transfer safeguards and information where required. **UK review required:** confirm any UK GDPR international transfer mechanism, whether Article 27 representative requirements apply or an exemption is available, and any UK-specific notice obligations. Do not publish an unsupported exemption or invented representative.

## 8. Your rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access, correction, deletion, or other rights relating to personal information we control. UK users may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, data portability where applicable, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). US state privacy rights may apply depending on state law and the business's applicability thresholds. For requests, contact **puphiicount@gmail.com**. Locally stored records can be managed on your device; we generally cannot access them remotely. We will respond within applicable legal time limits.

## 9. Children
Puphii is not designed for children under 13. **Owner review required:** establish the App Store age rating, age-access approach, and any additional age restrictions applicable to nicotine-related content and Voice Pep Rally. Do not claim an implemented age gate until verified.

## 10. Changes and contact
We may update this policy, publish a revised date, and provide additional notice or seek consent when required. Privacy inquiries: **puphiicount@gmail.com**. Operator: Naoko Hamasaki, trading as 39 Daisies, Oita Prefecture, Japan.
